About Futureproofing
Futureproofing builds AI-native founding teams for companies creating new products, launching new ventures, or transforming existing businesses around AI.
Our engineers work alongside founders, product leaders, data scientists, and business teams from problem definition through architecture, launch, and iteration.
We’re looking for builders who can take ownership of ambiguous problems and turn them into excellent production products—not engineers waiting for a perfectly defined ticket.

What You’ll Do
- Own AI products and features from problem definition through production

- Work directly with founders, executives, and subject-matter experts

- Design and build systems using LLMs, agents, retrieval, structured data, APIs, and traditional software

- Make thoughtful tradeoffs across quality, evaluation, latency, cost, reliability, and complexity

- Build evaluation and feedback loops that tell us whether AI systems are actually working

- Move quickly from prototype to production without sacrificing engineering quality

- Push back when a technical or product direction doesn’t make sense—and explain why

- Use AI deeply in your own engineering workflow while applying strong judgment and guardrails


What We’re Looking For
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience

- Experience owning and shipping real production products

- Meaningful experience building AI-powered products in production

- Strong Python and/or TypeScript/JavaScript skills with comfort across the stack

- Experience with modern AI architectures such as agents, tool use, RAG/retrieval, structured generation, and evaluation

- Strong product judgment and comfort operating with significant autonomy

- Clear communication with both technical and nontechnical stakeholders

- Genuine curiosity about AI—you actively experiment with new tools and continuously evolve how you build


We especially value engineers with 0→1 experience, startup backgrounds, customer exposure, or ownership beyond a narrow technical specialty.
You don’t need to know everything. You do need to be rigorous and deeply curious.

How We Work
Futureproofing is fully remote, with engineering talent primarily across Latin America and clients primarily in the United States.
Engagements may be part-time or full-time, with some US working-hour overlap. You’ll work closely with Futureproofing’s engineering leadership while working on a dedicated client founding team.

What Makes Futureproofing Different
Small, senior teams. We build teams around problems, not seats.
Real ownership. Engineers participate in architecture, product decisions, tradeoffs, and client conversations—not just implementation.
Production AI. We care about systems that survive contact with real users, data, and business constraints.
AI-native engineering. We expect our engineers to experiment constantly with how AI changes the way software is built.
Intentional matching. We get to know our engineers and clients deeply rather than matching through keywords.
Developer-first. We’d rather build a smaller network of exceptional engineers doing meaningful work than optimize for placement volume.
