Job Title: Remote AI Engineer ( 100% Work From Home) 
 
 Location: Remote from Latin America 
 
 Position Type: Full-time

 Schedule: Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM LATAM hours with lunch break. 

 About the Client 

 Our Client is a tech-forward platform company where AI agents serve as core operational infrastructure rather than ancillary features. Designing and deploying multi-agent systems directly on top of their core application, our client automates complex customer-facing and internal workflows—ranging from customer access support and user onboarding to WhatsApp conversational streams, real-time voice/call interactions, and business intelligence analytics. Operating with rigorous production standards, our client prioritizes system reliability, automated evaluation pipelines, low-latency streaming, and robust observability for high-volume customer interactions. 

 About the Role 

 Our Client is seeking a production-focused Senior AI Engineer to design, build, and operate multi-agent systems at scale. This is a hands-on execution role for a seasoned engineer who treats AI systems with the same engineering rigor as traditional mission-critical infrastructure. Reporting directly to engineering leadership, this professional will architect multi-agent handoffs, optimize low-latency streaming pipelines (including voice and WhatsApp channels), build automated evaluation frameworks, and instrument end-to-end observability across all live conversational agents. 

 Key Responsibilities 
 
 Multi-Agent System Architecture & Orchestration 

 Designs and deploys multi-agent orchestration frameworks on top of existing product infrastructure, establishing clear agent roles, tool access permissions, memory structures, and handoff protocols. 

 Builds low-latency, responsive streaming architectures that power real-time conversational agents across text, WhatsApp, and live voice/call pipelines. 

 Integrates autonomous agents with internal APIs, databases, and business intelligence dashboards to execute complex, multi-step customer workflows seamlessly. 

 Evaluation, Testing & Quality Assurance 

 Owns the end-to-end evaluation lifecycle, designing automated scoring pipelines, test datasets, and \"LLM-as-judge\" setups to prevent hallucinations, quality regressions, and drift. 

 Implements robust safety guardrails and boundary constraints for autonomous agents making real-time decisions or holding live conversations with minimal human oversight. 

 Debugs, troubleshoots, and refines non-deterministic agent outputs across live production environments to maintain high accuracy and customer satisfaction. 

 Observability, Security & Full Lifecycle Operations 

 Instruments comprehensive observability suites, tracking structured logs, detailed agent execution traces, tool calls, token costs, latency distributions, and accuracy metrics. 

 Manages the complete agent feature lifecycle—from prompt engineering and system design to API integration, unit testing, CI/CD deployment, and production monitoring. 

 Collaborates closely with product and core software engineering teams to ensure clean, modular integration into the existing application codebase without service disruption. 

 Qualifications, Skills, and Key Competencies 
 
 Required Qualifications 

 Production Multi-Agent Experience: Proven, hands-on background building and orchestrating multi-agent LLM architectures in live, user-facing production environments (not limited to prototypes, personal projects, or hackathons). 

 Agent System Design Mastery: Deep understanding of agent design patterns, including role separation, coordination/handoff logic, function calling/tool usage, long/short-term memory management, and advanced RAG architectures. 

 Streaming & Low-Latency Architecture: Demonstrated expertise in building low-latency, streaming data architectures for real-time responsiveness. 

 Systematic Evaluation Frameworks: Practical experience establishing automated testing, regression detection pipelines, and quality scoring systems for non-deterministic AI outputs. 

 Observability & Telemetry: Hands-on experience with LLM tracing, structured logging, cost tracking, and performance dashboards (e.g., LangSmith, Phoenix, Arize, Datadog). 

 Core Software Engineering: Exceptional software development fundamentals, clean code practices, API integration expertise, and database fluency. 

 Advanced English Proficiency: Excellent written and spoken English skills to collaborate effectively with cross-functional engineering teams. 

 Remote Infrastructure: Dedicated home workspace with high-performance computing hardware, reliable power redundancy, and high-speed internet. 

 Preferred Qualifications 

 Direct prior experience building voice/call agents, including real-time Speech-to-Text (STT), Text-to-Speech (TTS) pipelines, or telephony integrations. 

 Experience architecting analytics/BI agents capable of reasoning over structured data models and business dashboards. 

 Prior technical background in customer support workflows, CRM systems, or enterprise SaaS operations. 

 Benefits

 Schedule Alignment: Full-time stability tailored to standard LATAM business hours. 

 100% Remote Infrastructure: Work fully remotely from anywhere in Latin America.
